Le Classique is the name given to matches between Paris Saint-Germain and Olympique de Marseille, a name modelled on El Clásico between Real Madrid and Barcelona. The first meeting took place on 12 December 1971 at the Stade Vélodrome, where Marseille beat a newly formed PSG 4–2. The rivalry only really took hold from 1986, when PSG won their first French title and Bernard Tapie took over Marseille, and it intensified in 1991, when Canal+ bought PSG, partly to challenge Marseille's dominance. The fixture is often described as Paris against Marseille, the capital against the provinces, the north against the south. PSG have played at the Parc des Princes since 1974, while Marseille play at the Stade Vélodrome.
