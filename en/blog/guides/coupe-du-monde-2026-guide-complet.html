Format and Schedule

Group stage (11–27 June): 12 groups of 4 teams. The top 2 in each group plus the 8 best third-placed teams qualified → 32 teams in the round of 32.

Knockout stage (28 June – 19 July): round of 32, round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals (14 and 15 July), third-place play-off (18 July) and the Final on 19 July at MetLife Stadium, won by Spain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where did the 2026 World Cup take place?
The 2026 World Cup took place in the United States, Canada and Mexico from 11 June to 19 July 2026, in 16 host cities including New York, Los Angeles, Mexico City and Toronto.
How many teams took part in the 2026 World Cup?
48 teams took part, a first in history — compared with 32 at previous editions. They were split into 12 groups of 4, with 104 matches in total.
Does IASHARK predict the winner of the 2026 World Cup?
No. The IASHARK engine calculates probabilities match by match, based on real data available before each fixture — it does not issue a probability for the overall winner of a tournament, and published none for the 2026 World Cup.
Who won the 2026 World Cup and where was the final played?
Spain won the 2026 World Cup, beating Argentina 1-0 after extra time (Ferran Torres, 106th minute) on 19 July 2026 at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey (on the outskirts of New York). It was their second world title after 2010.
Can you bet on the winner of a World Cup?
Bets on the overall winner are offered by bookmakers, but IASHARK does not recommend any bet on this market: our engine only analyses individual matches, never the outcome of an entire tournament.
How did France do at the 2026 World Cup?
France, who qualified and were seen before the tournament as one of the historic favourites, lost 2-0 to Spain in the semi-final and then 6-4 to England in the third-place play-off, finishing fourth.
