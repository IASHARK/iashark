Real Madrid: fixtures, form and match statistics

Real Madrid were founded as Madrid Football Club and officially established on 6 March 1902. The club has played its home matches at the Bernabéu since 1947 and is known as Los Blancos, after the white home kit worn since its early days. Real Madrid shaped the early history of the European Cup by winning five consecutive editions from 1956 to 1960, and in 2014 became the first team to reach ten European titles, the win known in Spain as La Décima. Their defining domestic fixture is El Clásico against Barcelona, first played in 1902.
