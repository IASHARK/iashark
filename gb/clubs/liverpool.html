Liverpool: fixtures, form and match stats

Liverpool Football Club was founded in 1892, after a dispute led Everton to leave Anfield; the new club took over the ground and has played there ever since. That shared beginning is the root of the Merseyside derby, first played as a league fixture on 13 October 1894. Known as the Reds, a name that stuck for good when the team switched to an all-red home kit in 1964, Liverpool won the European Cup for the first time in 1977 under Bob Paisley, and You'll Never Walk Alone has been part of the Anfield atmosphere since the Bill Shankly era. Every day this page pulls together Liverpool's next fixtures with UK kick-off times, their Premier League position, their latest results and, when a match has already been analysed by IASHARK, an “Analysis available” label (the analysis itself is on the match page) with a link to the analysis.
