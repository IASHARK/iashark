The Soweto Derby is the meeting of Kaizer Chiefs and Orlando Pirates, two Premiership clubs based in Soweto, Johannesburg. It was first contested on 24 January 1970. The rivalry comes from the way Chiefs were born: Kaizer Motaung, a former Orlando Pirates star, returned from playing for the Atlanta Chiefs in the North American Soccer League, found infighting in the Pirates hierarchy and formed a Kaizer XI that played friendlies before evolving into Kaizer Chiefs. Chiefs use FNB Stadium for home matches, while Pirates play at Orlando Stadium. On this page IASHARK tracks the derby with data that is refreshed every day: the next scheduled meeting when the fixture is known, the most recent head-to-head results, where both clubs sit on the PSL log and their current form. When the derby has a published IASHARK analysis, you will also find an “Analysis available” label (the analysis itself is on the match page) and a link to the match page.
