THE METHOD

Four models, one signal per match.

MODEL 1
Poisson
Distribution of expected goals for each team, home and away.
MODEL 2
Dixon-Coles
Correction for low scores (0-0, 1-0, 1-1) that pure Poisson underestimates.
MODEL 3
Monte Carlo
Thousands of match simulations to get a robust distribution of outcomes.
MODEL 4
Elo & Shin
Real team strength over time, and correction of the implied-probability bias.
IN PRACTICE

From raw data to a usable signal.

01
We ingest the data
Schedules, probable lineups, head-to-head history and xG stats fed in continuously, league by league.
02
The model computes
Poisson, Dixon-Coles and Monte Carlo run in parallel to produce a probability distribution for each market.
03
You get the signal
A clear sheet per match: probability, reliability level, and the market where the model detects a gap with the real market.
