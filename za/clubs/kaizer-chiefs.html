Kaizer Chiefs: fixtures, stats and analysis

Kaizer Chiefs were founded on 7 January 1970 by Kaizer Motaung, a former Orlando Pirates player who had played in the North American Soccer League for the Atlanta Chiefs and combined his own first name with that club's to name the new team. Known as Amakhosi, a Zulu word for kings or chiefs, the club is based in Naturena, Johannesburg South, and plays its home matches at FNB Stadium. That origin story is also why the Soweto Derby against Orlando Pirates, first played on 24 January 1970, carries so much weight. This page is refreshed every day from real match data.
