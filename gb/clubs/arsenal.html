Arsenal: fixtures, form and match stats

Arsenal were formed in October 1886, when David Danskin and fifteen fellow munitions workers in Woolwich set up Dial Square, renamed Royal Arsenal by early 1887; the Gunners nickname and the cannon on the crest still point back to those origins. The club crossed the river to Highbury in 1913, a move that made Tottenham their nearest neighbours and started the North London derby, and has played at Emirates Stadium since 2006. Arsenal's best-known modern landmark is the 2003–04 Premier League season, completed unbeaten by the side remembered as the Invincibles.
