IASHARK is not a bookmaker

IASHARK publishes statistical analyses of football matches. We do not accept wagers, place bets for you or hold gambling funds, and we do not hold a Gambling Commission licence.

18+ only

IASHARK is only for adults aged 18 or over. The minimum legal age for most forms of gambling in Great Britain, including online betting, is 18.

A probability is not a promise

Our analyses are estimates. An outcome rated at 60% will, on average, not happen four times out of ten, and a good run tells you nothing certain about the next result. Nothing on IASHARK guarantees winnings, and betting is not a way to make an income.

Keeping it under control

  • Set a budget and a time limit before you start, and stick to them.
  • Only bet what you can afford to lose, and never borrow to gamble.
  • Do not chase losses.
  • Take breaks, and avoid gambling when you are stressed, low or have been drinking.
  • Use the deposit limits, time-outs and reality checks offered by licensed operators.
  • Only bet with operators licensed by the Gambling Commission — you can check its public register at gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register.

Warning signs

  • Spending more money or time on gambling than you intended.
  • Hiding your gambling or lying about it.
  • Gambling causing debt, arguments, or problems at work or study.
  • Feeling anxious, irritable or low when you are not gambling.

If any of these sound familiar, support is free and confidential.

Free support

National Gambling Helpline — 0808 8020 133
Free, confidential advice and support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, run by GamCare. Live chat is also available at gamcare.org.uk.

  • GAMSTOP — a free self-exclusion service that blocks your access to gambling websites and apps licensed in Great Britain: gamstop.co.uk.
  • GambleAware (formerly BeGambleAware) — information, self-assessment tools and a finder for free treatment and support services: gambleaware.org.
  • Gambling Therapy — free online support from the charity Gordon Moody, wherever you live: gamblingtherapy.org.
  • If you are in immediate danger, call 999.
