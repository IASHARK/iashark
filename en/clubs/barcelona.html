FC Barcelona: fixtures, form and match statistics

FC Barcelona were founded on 29 November 1899 by Swiss-born Joan Gamper and a group of football enthusiasts who answered an advertisement he had placed. The club's motto, Més que un club (more than a club), reflects the role it plays in Catalan culture and identity, and Barcelona is owned and run by its members rather than by an individual owner. Its La Masia academy, set up in 1979 in a historic farmhouse used as a residence for young players, produced players such as Xavi, Andrés Iniesta and Lionel Messi. In 2009 Barcelona became the first Spanish club to win the treble of La Liga, Copa del Rey and Champions League in the same season.
