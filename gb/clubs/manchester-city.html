Manchester City: fixtures, form and match stats

Manchester City were founded in 1880 as St. Mark's (West Gorton), became Ardwick in 1887 and took the Manchester City name on 16 April 1894, the year from which the club has worn sky blue at home. After playing at Maine Road from 1923, City moved to the City of Manchester Stadium in east Manchester in 2003, now known as the Etihad Stadium. Their 2022–23 season brought a continental treble of Premier League, FA Cup and Champions League, and they won four consecutive league titles from 2020–21 to 2023–24, an English record. Their oldest rivalry is the Manchester derby against United, whose first meeting dates back to 1881.
