Mamelodi Sundowns: fixtures, stats and analysis

Mamelodi Sundowns took their current name in 1970 and are based in Mamelodi, Pretoria, in Gauteng. They play home games at Loftus Versfeld Stadium and are nicknamed Masandawana and The Brazilians, the second after the club changed its kit to resemble that of Brazil's national team. In 2016 Sundowns beat Egypt's Zamalek 3–1 on aggregate to win the CAF Champions League, their first continental title, and became only the second South African club to be crowned champions of Africa.
