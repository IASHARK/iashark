Inter Milan: fixtures, form and match statistics

Inter were founded on 9 March 1908 after a split within the Milan Foot-Ball and Cricket Club, driven by members who wanted to accept foreign players. Known as the Nerazzurri for their black and blue colours, Inter have shared the San Siro, officially the Stadio Giuseppe Meazza, with AC Milan since 1947, and the two clubs contest the Derby della Madonnina. Inter have been in Italy's top division without interruption since 1909 and have never been relegated to Serie B; in 2010 they became the first Italian club to win the league, the cup and the European Cup in the same season. Their long-running rivalry with Juventus is known as the Derby d'Italia.
