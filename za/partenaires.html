IASHARK Partner programme

40% of every subscription, for life.

Do you talk football to a community? Recommend IASHARK with your link or your code. Every time one of your subscribers pays for their Pro plan — weekly, monthly or yearly — you earn 40% of the amount collected. Not just the first time: on every payment, for as long as they stay subscribed.

How it works

  1. 1

    Create your IASHARK account and fill in the partner questionnaire (your networks, your audience, your country).

  2. 2

    IASHARK approves your application. You get your link, your code and a QR code.

  3. 3

    Your subscribers sign up with your link or type your code at checkout. On every payment collected, your commission appears in your space; it becomes payable 30 days later and is paid out monthly from 50 in your currency.

The rules, in plain words

  • Single tier: you are paid on your subscribers, never on partners you bring in.
  • Nothing during the free trial: the commission starts with the first real payment.
  • Refund or chargeback: the matching commission is taken back.
  • Every piece of content must carry the words "paid partnership" or "advertisement", address an audience aged 18 and over, and never promise winnings or say how much to play.
  • No commission on yourself, your own cards or your own addresses: such attempts are detected and lead to suspension.
