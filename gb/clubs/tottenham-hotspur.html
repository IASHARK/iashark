Tottenham Hotspur: fixtures, form and match stats

Tottenham Hotspur were founded on 5 September 1882 as Hotspur F.C. and spent well over a century at White Hart Lane, from 1899 until the ground was demolished in 2017. The club moved into Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on 3 April 2019. Spurs achieved the first league and FA Cup Double of the 20th century in 1960–61 and, in 1963, became the first British team to win a European trophy, beating Atlético Madrid 5–1 in the Cup Winners' Cup final. Their rivalry with Arsenal took shape after Arsenal moved to Highbury, four miles away, in 1913.
