THE METHOD

Four models, one probability per match.

MODEL 1
Poisson
Distribution of expected goals for each team, home and away.
MODEL 2
Dixon-Coles
Correction for low scores (0-0, 1-0, 1-1) that pure Poisson underestimates.
MODEL 3
Monte Carlo
Thousands of match simulations to get a robust distribution of outcomes.
MODEL 4
Elo & Shin
Real team strength over time, and correction of the implied-probability bias.
METHOD, END TO END

From raw data to a public record.

01
Data
Schedules, probable lineups, head-to-head history and expected-goals stats for leagues including the Premier League, the Champions League and LaLiga, ingested continuously.
02
Models
Poisson, Dixon-Coles and Monte Carlo run in parallel to produce a probability distribution for each market.
03
Market comparison
The model's probability is set next to the market-implied price, so any gap between the two is visible.
04
Public record
The forecast is published before kickoff and kept on the record, win or lose.

FAQ

Before you sign up.

Does IAShark guarantee any result?
No. Every number is a statistical estimate, not a promise. No outcome is certain, and past results don't guarantee future ones.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Pro and Edge are billed monthly and can be cancelled anytime from your account. Annual Edge follows the same subscription terms, billed once a year.
What data does the model use?
Public football data: schedules, lineups, head-to-head history and expected-goals statistics, plus market prices where available. No personal betting activity is required to use it.
What are the limits of the model?
It estimates probabilities from historical and statistical data. It cannot account for every real-world factor, and like any statistical estimate, it can be wrong.
Who is IAShark for, and what about responsible gambling?
Adults aged 18 and over in South Africa. IAShark is a subscription analytics service — it does not accept wagers, does not hold gambling funds and is not a bookmaker. If gambling is affecting you or someone you know, the National Responsible Gambling Programme (see footer) offers free, confidential support.
