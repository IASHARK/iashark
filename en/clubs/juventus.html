Juventus: fixtures, form and match statistics

Juventus were founded in Turin on 1 November 1897 by students, as Sport-Club Juventus. The club has played at its own stadium since 2011, known commercially since 2017 as Allianz Stadium. Juventus hold the record for Italian league titles and are known as the Bianconeri, the white and blacks, and as la Vecchia Signora, the Old Lady, a play on a Latin name that means youth. Their city rivalry, the Derby della Mole, goes back to 1906, when Torino were founded by players and staff who had broken away from Juventus, while the Derby d'Italia against Inter is their most high-profile rivalry.
