1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

A cookie or similar technology is a piece of information stored in, or read from, your browser when you visit a website: a cookie, local storage ("localStorage") or session storage. This policy describes the technologies used on iashark.com and how you can manage them.

To understand how the service is used and improve it, IASHARK records the main steps of the user journey (visit to a key page, sign-up, opening checkout…) in its own database. No third-party tool, third-party cookie or advertising pixel is used for this measurement.

Google Analytics cookies (a service provided by Google Ireland Limited) are only set if you click "Accept" on the banner shown on your first visit. They measure site traffic (pages viewed, session duration).

5. Third-party resources

Some pages load resources hosted by third parties: fonts (Google Fonts), a technical library (jsDelivr) and team logos (API-SPORTS). Loading them transmits your IP address to these providers. Payment takes place on Stripe's secure page, which uses its own technologies under its privacy policy.

6. Managing your choices

  • Banner — on your first visit, you can accept or refuse analytics cookies. Refusing has no effect on your access to the service.
  • Changing your mind — clear this site's browsing data in your browser: the banner will be shown again on your next visit.
  • Browser settings — you can block or delete cookies and local storage; blocking strictly necessary storage may prevent you from logging in to your account.
