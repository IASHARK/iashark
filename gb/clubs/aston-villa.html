Aston Villa: fixtures, form and match stats

Aston Villa were founded in 1874 by members of the Aston Villa Wesleyan Chapel and played a pioneering role in the professional game: Villa director William McGregor set up the Football League in 1888, with Villa among its twelve founder members. The club has played at Villa Park since 1897, the largest football stadium in the English Midlands. Its greatest European night came in the 1982 European Cup final, a 1–0 win over Bayern Munich. Villa's local rivalries are the Second City derby with Birmingham City, contested since 1879, and the West Midlands derby with West Bromwich Albion.
