El Clásico: Real Madrid vs Barcelona

El Clásico is the name given to any match between Barcelona and Real Madrid, whether it is played in La Liga, the Champions League, the Copa del Rey or the Supercopa de España; in Catalan it is El Clàssic. The first meeting took place on 13 May 1902 in the semi-final round of the Copa de la Coronación, at Madrid's Hipódromo de la Castellana, and Barcelona won 3–1. Real Madrid, officially established that same year, have played at the Bernabéu since 1947. Barcelona, founded in 1899 by Joan Gamper, are owned and run by their members.
