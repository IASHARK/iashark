What IASHARK is — and is not

IASHARK is a subscription-based football statistics service. It publishes probabilities estimated by statistical models and compares them with prices available on the betting market.

IASHARK is not a bookmaker or a gambling operator. We do not accept bets, we do not place bets on behalf of users, we do not hold gambling funds and we do not provide any gambling activity. IASHARK does not hold a licence from the National Gambling Board, from any provincial licensing authority or from any other gambling regulator.

Our analyses are statistical estimates provided for information only. A probability is not a certainty: nothing on this site guarantees any result or winnings, and nothing on it is financial advice.
