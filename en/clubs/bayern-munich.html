Bayern Munich: fixtures, form and match statistics

FC Bayern Munich were founded on 27 February 1900 by eleven players and have played at the Allianz Arena since the start of the 2005–06 season, after leaving Munich's Olympiastadion. Bayern hold the record for German championships, a run that includes eleven consecutive titles from 2013 to 2023. In Europe, the club won the European Cup three times in a row between 1974 and 1976 and completed trebles in 2012–13 and again in 2020. Their best-known domestic rivalry is Der Klassiker against Borussia Dortmund.
