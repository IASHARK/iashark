THE METHOD

Four steps, one signal per match.

STEP 1
Team strengths
What each team produces and concedes in its real situation, at home or away, compared with the level of its competition.
STEP 2
Caution early in the season
When few matches have been played, the previous season and a reference level weigh more, then fade away.
STEP 3
Probability of every market
Expected goals become the probability of every scoreline, then of every market, corrected by a calibration learnt from past seasons.
STEP 4
Match simulation
The match is replayed a large number of times to bring out the most likely scorelines. The simulation does not choose the bet.
