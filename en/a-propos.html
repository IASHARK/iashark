IASHARK analyses every match using sports data built up over several seasons and rigorous statistical models. No martingale, no miracle system — one method, applied in exactly the same way, match after match.

METHODOLOGY

How the model works

For each match, our system draws on an extensive history of real data: team and player results and statistics across several seasons, recent form trends, and contextual factors (probable line-ups, absences, fixture schedule). This history feeds several complementary statistical models, whose results are systematically cross-checked before any conclusion is drawn — never a single model in isolation, never a hunch.

MULTI-SEASON HISTORY
Every calculation is based on several seasons of real results and statistics, not on a one-off impression or the latest headlines.
CROSS-CHECKED MODELS
Several independent statistical models analyse each fixture in parallel; a conclusion is only kept when they converge.
SIMULATIONS
Thousands of match scenarios are simulated for each fixture, to check that the results obtained are statistically consistent.
RELIABILITY SCORE
A separate indicator measures how closely the models agree and the quality of the data available for that specific match — it never rewrites the probability itself.

The market highlighted for each match is selected automatically according to deterministic statistical criteria, with no manual intervention. The probability shown is the model's own, never adjusted after the fact. A language model then writes the explanatory text that accompanies this choice, but it does not calculate, choose or modify any figure: the probabilities, odds, selected market and reliability level are determined entirely by our statistical method, never by that text.

The concept of value: our model compares its calculated probability with the odds offered by bookmakers. When our estimate differs significantly from the one implied by the odds, this is called a "value bet" — odds that the market may have mispriced. The exact details of our calculation method are proprietary and are not publicly disclosed.

READ THIS FIRST

One model per match, no combo bets

At its core, the system analyses each match independently — one single bet at a time, never combinations of several bets.
