Orlando Pirates: fixtures, stats and analysis

Orlando Pirates were founded in 1937 in Orlando, Soweto, and play their home matches at Orlando Stadium in the township where the club began. Supporters know them as the Buccaneers or simply the Bucs, as well as The Sea Robbers and The Happy People. On the continental stage, Pirates won the African Champions Cup, today's CAF Champions League, in 1995. At home, their defining fixture is the Soweto Derby against Kaizer Chiefs, a club formed by former Pirates player Kaizer Motaung; the two sides first met on 24 January 1970.
