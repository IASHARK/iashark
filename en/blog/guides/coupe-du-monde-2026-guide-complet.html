Format and Schedule

Group stage (11 June – 2 July): 12 groups of 4 teams. The top 2 plus the 8 best third-placed teams qualify → 32 teams in the round of 16.

Knockout stage (3–19 July): round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals, third-place play-off and the Final on 19 July at MetLife Stadium.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the 2026 World Cup take place?
The 2026 World Cup takes place in the United States, Canada and Mexico from 11 June to 19 July 2026. There are 16 host cities, including New York, Los Angeles, Mexico City and Toronto.
How many teams are taking part in the 2026 World Cup?
48 teams are taking part for the first time in history — compared with 32 at previous editions. They are split into 12 groups of 4, with 104 matches in total.
Does IASHARK predict the winner of the 2026 World Cup?
No. The IASHARK engine calculates probabilities match by match, based on real data available before each fixture — it does not issue a probability for the overall winner of a tournament. Match-by-match analyses of the 2026 World Cup will be available as the tournament progresses, once the fixture schedule has been set.
Where will the 2026 World Cup final be played?
The final will take place at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey (on the outskirts of New York) on 19 July 2026. It is the largest stadium used for the competition, with a capacity of 82,500.
Can you bet on the winner of the 2026 World Cup?
Bets on the overall winner are offered by bookmakers, but IASHARK does not recommend any bet on this market: our engine only analyses individual matches, never the outcome of an entire tournament.
Have France qualified for the 2026 World Cup?
Yes, France have qualified and are among the historic favourites for the tournament. The French side are aiming to triumph on the American continent after their 2018 title in Russia.
