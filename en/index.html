FREQUENTLY ASKED

What you're wondering before you pay.

Can I try before paying?+

Yes. With a free account, no card required, you can read the analysis of the free match. That match is picked by a fixed rule of the model, written in advance. Some days, there is no free match.

Does IASHARK guarantee winnings?+

No, and nobody can. The model estimates probabilities, then compares them with the odds. It is a method, not a guarantee.

How is the signal computed?+

The system estimates each team's expected goals, derives the probability of every scoreline and then of every market, and keeps the most likely market, above a minimum price and below a probability cap. The gap with the market price is computed afterwards and shown separately.

What happens when the data isn't enough?+

No signal is published for that match. It is stated explicitly rather than replaced by a filler bet.

Which competitions are covered?+

48 competitions, including Ligue 1, the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, the Bundesliga, the Champions League, Liga MX, the Premier Soccer League and the top leagues of Argentina, Colombia, Peru and Chile.

Can I cancel any time?+

Yes, online from your account, at any time. No commitment, no notice, no reason needed.

How often are the analyses updated?+

The bet is locked as soon as it is published: neither the bet, nor its probability, nor its odds change afterwards. Until kick-off, only the match facts (line-ups, absences, bookmaker odds) are still updated.

HOW IT WORKS

Football predictions built on probabilities, not hunches

IASHARK produces football predictions from a statistical score model. For the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, the Bundesliga, Ligue 1, the Champions League and the other competitions we cover, the model estimates the probability of each result, the number of goals and both teams to score, then compares it with the probability implied by bookmaker odds.

These are statistical estimates: football is uncertain, no outcome is guaranteed and IASHARK is not a bookmaker. Data coverage varies by league, so every analysis shows its data-quality rating. 18+ only.
