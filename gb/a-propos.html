METHODOLOGY

How the model works

Every analysis is produced automatically, in four steps, from gathering the data to writing the text.

DATA
Team and player statistics, match history and the odds offered by bookmakers.
STATISTICAL MODEL
A model uses this data to estimate the chances of the main outcomes of each match.
FIXED RULE
The highlighted option is chosen automatically among the markets actually priced, favouring the most likely outcomes.
AI-WRITTEN TEXT
An artificial intelligence tool writes the explanation from figures already calculated and cannot change them.

A match that has already started gets no option, and no option is highlighted when the data is insufficient. The probability shown is a statistical estimate, never a promise.

How to read the figures: 60% means the outcome should happen about 6 times out of 10 over a large number of comparable matches, and not happen about 4 times out of 10. No winnings are guaranteed.

What we do not detail: the exact sources, the formulas, the settings or the tools used. What we do publish: the principle, how to read the figures and the limits of the system.
