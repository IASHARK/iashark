Frequently Asked Questions

Which bookmakers are legal in France in 2026?
In France, only operators licensed by the ANJ (Autorité Nationale des Jeux, the French gambling regulator) may legally offer online sports betting. These include Betclic, Winamax, Unibet, PMU and Bet365. Always check that the site states its ANJ licence before creating an account.
How can you check whether a bookmaker is legal in France?
Consult the official list of licensed operators on the ANJ website (anj.fr). A legal operator clearly displays its licence number and only accepts accounts from adults.
What are the risks of betting on a site that is not licensed in France?
Betting with an operator that is not licensed in France offers no legal protection in the event of a dispute, a refusal to pay out winnings or manipulation, and the ANJ cannot intervene with an operator outside its jurisdiction.
Which licensed operator offers the best odds in France?
Odds vary by league and change constantly. Rather than relying on a fixed ranking, it is better to compare several licensed operators (Winamax, Betclic, Unibet, Bet365, PMU) at the time of any bet.
